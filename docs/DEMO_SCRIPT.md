# Two-minute demo script

**Target length:** about 1 minute 35 seconds of speaking, with brief pauses to show the screens.

## Before the demo

Open these three links in separate tabs and let the match finish in each tab. The high replay speed gets you to the full-time recap quickly; the saved demo runs make the cards appear without waiting for a model.

- Fan, following Fox: [Fan demo](https://inside-the-game.calmocean-9ce9113c.eastus2.azurecontainerapps.io/?role=fan&club=Red+Lions&player=Fox&focus=1&speed=120)
- Analyst: [Analyst demo](https://inside-the-game.calmocean-9ce9113c.eastus2.azurecontainerapps.io/?role=analyst&speed=120)
- Broadcaster: [Broadcaster demo](https://inside-the-game.calmocean-9ce9113c.eastus2.azurecontainerapps.io/?role=broadcaster&speed=120)

## Spoken script

**[0:00 — Fan tab; point to the pitch and player-follow indicator]**

“This is *Inside the Game*. Instead of stopping at the score, it turns synthetic match events into a live story. Here, I’m following the Red Lions and Fox. The pitch, player tags, clock and commentary are all synchronized to the replay.”

**[0:20 — Point to a commentary card and the timeline]**

“Key moments get more time on screen, and the commentary is personalized. Fans can choose a club, a player to follow, and a language. The numbers are computed from the event data, and generated claims are checked before they’re shown.”

**[0:42 — Switch to Analyst tab; point to live numbers and an evidence card]**

“The analyst gets a different experience: live xG, possession, passing and tempo, plus evidence explaining why a moment matters—not just what happened. They can also track a preferred metric.”

**[1:02 — Switch to Broadcaster tab; point to lower third and director cues; open JSON feed]**

“For the studio, each moment can produce a lower third, a talking point and a prioritized director cue. The overlay feed is structured JSON, ready for a graphics system.”

**[1:22 — Switch back to the Fan tab at full time]**

“At full time, viewers get a recap, and three specialist perspectives are combined into one fact-checked report. One match, told differently for fans, analysts and broadcasters.”

## If time is tight

Keep the three tabs pre-opened, skip the JSON dialog, and move directly between the three views. The spoken portion is about 150 words; leave the screens visible while switching.
